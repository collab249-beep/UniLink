# Build a Mobile App Called "UniLink"

## Overview

Build a modern mobile app called UniLink** that helps university students meet other students in real life instantly.**

The app's purpose is to reduce loneliness, encourage social interaction, and help students find people to do activities with nearby.

Unlike social media, UniLink should prioritize real-world meetings instead of online conversations.

The experience should feel fast, simple, and frictionless.

---

# Core Concept

A student opens the app, selects what they want to do right now, and is automatically matched with a small group of nearby students who want to do the same activity.

The goal is to go from opening the app to having a meetup arranged in under 60 seconds.

---

# Target Users

* University students
* College students
* Freshers and international students
* Students looking to make friends
* Students looking for study partners

---

# Authentication

Keep onboarding extremely simple.

Users can:

* Sign up with email
* Sign up with phone number
* Sign in with Google

University verification is required.

Users must verify using a university email address before joining activities.

Examples:

* .edu
* .ac.uk

No lengthy onboarding process.

---

# Home Screen

Display only six activity options:

🍔 Get Food

📚 Study

⚽ Sports

🎮 Chill

☕ Coffee

🎵 Events

Design should be large, clean, and easy to tap.

No feeds.

No notifications page.

No content browsing.

---

# Activity Matching

When a user selects an activity:

1. Capture current location.
2. Search for nearby users doing the same activity.
3. Match users within a configurable radius.
4. Automatically create a group.

Group size:

* Minimum: 2 people
* Maximum: 6 people

Do not allow users to browse profiles.

Do not allow users to choose specific people.

The system should automatically create the best available group.

---

# Meetup Creation

Once a group is formed:

* Generate a meetup session automatically.
* Suggest a nearby meeting location.
* Display a countdown timer.

Example:

"Meet at Campus Café"

"Meet within 30 minutes"

Display:

* Meeting location
* Time remaining
* Number of participants

---

# Session Lifecycle

After joining:

Status = Active

Countdown = 30 minutes

Users can confirm attendance.

If users repeatedly fail to attend meetups, reduce their reliability score.

Sessions automatically expire after 60 minutes.

Expired sessions disappear from the app.

---

# Safety Features

Include:

* Report User
* Block User
* Leave Meetup
* Emergency Help Button

Safety actions should always be accessible during active sessions.

---

# User Profiles

Keep profiles minimal.

Display only:

* First Name
* University
* Profile Picture

Do NOT include:

* Bios
* Followers
* Following
* Posts
* Likes
* Messaging history

---

# Messaging Rules

No chat before meeting.

Users should only see:

"You have been matched."

"Meet at Campus Café in 30 minutes."

The focus is real-world interaction, not online conversation.

---

# Success Metric

The primary success metric is:

"How quickly can a student go from opening the app to meeting another student in real life?"

Optimize every screen and feature around this goal.
